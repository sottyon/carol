How to use this site

  1. On the Options tab, browse each destination and place to stay. Use the People buttons (1, 3, 5, 7 or 10) to see what the trip costs for your group size.
  2. Tap the heart on up to 3 places you like to build a personal shortlist. This is just for you and does not count as a vote.
  3. Open the Vote tab, enter your name and choose your top 3 destinations.
  4. Press Submit my vote. Your vote only counts once it is submitted.
  5. Check the Results tab to see how the destinations are ranking. You can come back and change your vote anytime.

Don’t forget to submit your vote, picking destinations alone isn’t enough.

Resort prices come from the Costco Travel Portal and include flights, food and airport transfer for the stay (per person, based on 2 adults). Villa and home prices are lodging per person for about 10 guests plus approximate flights; food is not included. Group-size prices are estimates: resort packages are multiplied per person, and for villas the lodging total (the 10-person price × 10) is split across the group while flights are added per traveler. Prices will change. Florida and Las Vegas homes are Airbnb listings with prices shown on Airbnb.
